This policy explains what synferrous.com (the "Site") stores in your browser and why. It sits alongside our [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

## The short version

- The Site **does not set cookies**.
- There is **no analytics, advertising or tracking**: no Google Analytics, no Meta pixel, no third-party tracking scripts.
- The only thing a visitor's browser may store is a random chat session ID, and only if you use the chat assistant.

Because nothing is used for tracking or advertising, we don't show a cookie banner. There is nothing optional to accept or reject.

## What we store, and why

We use your browser's **local storage**, which is similar to a cookie but is never sent to our servers automatically. It is used only for the items below.

**For visitors**

- `iris_session`: a random ID created when you send your first message to the chat assistant, when the assistant is switched on. It lets the conversation continue if you reload the page. It contains no personal details. It stays until you start a new conversation or clear your browser storage. The conversation itself is stored on our servers for the period set out in the Privacy Policy.

**For site administrators only**

- `ciq_token`: keeps an administrator signed in. Removed on sign-out.
- `rk_admin_nav_order` and `rk_admin_theme`: remember how an administrator has arranged the admin menu and whether they prefer light or dark mode.

All of these are strictly necessary for a feature you have chosen to use. None is shared with anyone or used to identify, profile or follow you across other websites.

## Content from other services

Some images on the Site are loaded from image hosting services (for example Unsplash and Pexels) or from our own image storage. As with any web request, those services receive your IP address and basic browser information so they can deliver the image. We don't use them to track you, and we haven't set them up to place cookies. If that ever changes, we will update this policy before it does.

Our hosting providers (Vercel for the website, Render for the server) keep standard server logs, including IP addresses, for security and reliability. These are logs, not cookies.

## Managing browser storage

You can view and clear local storage for synferrous.com at any time in your browser's settings, usually under **Privacy → Site data** or **Cookies and site data**. Clearing it simply resets the chat. Nothing else on the Site depends on it.

## If this changes

If we ever add analytics or any other non-essential cookies, we will ask for your consent first, through a clear choice that is as easy to refuse as to accept, and update this policy before they are used.
